Age 0 needs only wood, stone and copper. No iron.

{{image shots/codex_01_start.jpg|The Codex Next steps page}}

## Day one

1. **[[copper_gear]]**: 4 copper ingots + 1 cobblestone.
2. **[[winding_crank]]**: stick, 2 copper, a gear, 3 cobblestone.
3. **[[mainspring]]**: 8 copper around a gear. Holds 240,000 FE.
4. Put the Mainspring in the crank and **hold right-click** with an empty hand: 400 FE per turn, 5 turns/s. Full in about 2 minutes.
5. **[[clockwork_mechanism]]**: 4 gears, 4 planks, 1 cobblestone.
6. **[[wooden_chassis]]**: 4 logs, 4 cobblestone, 1 copper block.

{{items copper_gear winding_crank mainspring clockwork_mechanism wooden_chassis}}

## First robots

- [[stumpy]]: fells whole trees in a 7x7 area, replants, 250 FE per log.
- [[sprout]]: harvests and replants crops in a 9x9 area.
- Right-click the robot with the wound Mainspring (or a cell) to power it. Place a [[supply_crate]] (27 slots) next to the robot: it outputs there.
- Sneak-right-click with an empty hand: status and work area.

{{image shots/gui_11_stumpy.jpg|Stumpy's screen}}

## Plates by hand

- [[tinkers_hammer]] + 2 ingots in the grid = 1 plate (costs durability). Also mines 3x3.

## First iron hour

No gold or diamonds needed.

| Item | Does |
|---|---|
| [[combustion_generator]] | burns furnace fuel, 80 FE/t; right-click it with fuel |
| [[charger]] | charges FE items at 2,000 FE/t |
| [[copper_cell]] | 800,000 FE battery; recharges the tool in your hand |
| [[bore_drill]] | smithing: [[tool_upgrade_kit_1]] + Tinker's Hammer + [[electric_motor]]; 400k FE, 40 FE per block |

{{items combustion_generator charger copper_cell tool_upgrade_kit_1 bore_drill}}
