Age 0 needs only wood, stone and copper. No iron.

{{image shots/codex_01_start.jpg|The Codex Next steps page}}

## Day one

Make a [[copper_gear]], then a [[winding_crank]] and a [[mainspring]]. Wind the spring at the crank and right-click a robot with it. Your first robots: [[stumpy]] for wood, [[sprout]] for crops.

{{items copper_gear winding_crank mainspring clockwork_mechanism wooden_chassis stumpy sprout}}

{{image shots/gui_stumpy.jpg|Stumpy's screen}}

Until you have power, the [[tinkers_hammer]] makes plates and dust by hand.

## First iron hour

Build a [[combustion_generator]] and a [[charger]], then a [[copper_cell]]. Keep the cell in your inventory: it recharges the FE tool in your hand. Then turn the Tinker's Hammer into a [[bore_drill]].

{{items combustion_generator charger copper_cell tool_upgrade_kit_1 bore_drill}}
