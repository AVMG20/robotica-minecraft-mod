Three new ores, three alloys that only Robotica makes, and parts that only the Assembler builds.

{{items thorium_ore pyrolite_ore resonite_ore alloy_smelter_mk1 centrifuge_mk1 assembler_mk1 metal_press}}

## The chain

1. **Ore**: mine it (see [Finding ores](#/guide/finding-ores)), grind it in a [[grinder_mk1]] for double.
2. **Alloy**: [[alloy_smelter_mk1]], ingot or dust inputs.
3. **Plate**: [[metal_press]] (1 ingot) or [[tinkers_hammer]] (2 ingots).
4. **Part**: [[assembler_mk1]].

## Alloys

| Alloy | Age | Inputs | Time | Power |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [[ferrothorium_ingot]] | 2 | iron + thorium | 200 ticks | 40 FE/t |

| [[resonant_alloy_ingot]] | 4 | pyrosteel + resonite + [[minecraft:ender_pearl]] | 400 ticks | 400 FE/t |
