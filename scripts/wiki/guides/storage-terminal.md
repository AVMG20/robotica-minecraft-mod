## Setup

- No cables needed: a cell or wound Mainspring in its battery slot runs it for a long time.
- Pipes, hoppers and Courier Drones fill it and empty it from any side. Without power nothing goes in, so pipes feeding it back up.
- A comparator reads how full it is.

## Crafting

- JEI's **+** fills the grid from the terminal first, then from your inventory. Shift-click **+** fills as many crafts as the items allow.
- Shift-click the result: the crafted items go into the terminal first.
- The grid keeps its items when you close the screen.

## Terminal Link

- Charge it in a Charger before you leave.
- It must stay in your hand. Hold it in your off hand to keep the screen open while you use your hotbar.
