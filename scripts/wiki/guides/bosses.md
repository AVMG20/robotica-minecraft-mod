Each boss drops its age core every time and locks its loot to the killer for 2 minutes.

- A target they can't reach for 3 seconds gets hit from range, out to 40 blocks.
- With no player within 32 blocks for 30 seconds they heal.

## Scrap Colossus (Age 2)

{{items signal_flare colossus_altar servo_core}}

{{image shots/12_scrap_colossus.jpg|The Scrap Colossus on its altar}}

Find a Rusted Foundry (plains, deserts, badlands) or craft a [[colossus_altar]], then right-click it with a [[signal_flare]].

- Arms up: ground slam. Jump or back off. Arm back: thrown scrap.
- Below half health it calls Scrap Drones and overheats: hit the open furnace for double damage.

## Forge Tyrant (Age 3)

{{items ignition_charge forge_altar magma_core}}

{{image shots/27_forge_tyrant.jpg|The Forge Tyrant venting next to its Forge Altar}}

Find a Cinder Forge (blackstone ruins in Nether caves) or craft a [[forge_altar]], then right-click it with an [[ignition_charge]].

| Tell | Attack | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Furnace doors glow | Flame breath, a 7 block cone | Step out of the cone |
| Crucible arm swings back | Magma globs | Keep moving |
| Hammer goes up | Glowing rings, then eruptions | Get off the rings |
| Doors swing open | Vent, after every 3 attacks | Hit it: double damage |

> Below half health it rings itself with eruptions and attacks faster. Fire Resistance helps.
