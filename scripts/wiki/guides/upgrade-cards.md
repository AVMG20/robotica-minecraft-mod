Cards upgrade machines. A machine takes only the kinds it can use.

{{items upgrade_speed upgrade_efficiency upgrade_range upgrade_fortune upgrade_silk upgrade_growth upgrade_void upgrade_height upgrade_carry}}

## Install

- Right-click the machine with the card, or put it in an upgrade slot.
- One kind per slot. Stackable kinds stack in that slot: every card is one step.

## The Mk rule

Every machine with a Mk (Alloy Smelter, Centrifuge, Assembler, Grinder, Electric Furnace, Excavator, Survey Rig, Stumpy and Sprout with Farm Kits):

{{mkrule}}

Machines without a Mk (Metal Press, Combustion Generator, Wireless Charger, Replicator, Architect Table, drones) have fixed slots, listed below.

## The cards

| Card | Max | One step |
|---|---|---|
| [[upgrade_speed]] | 8 | x2, x3, x4, x6, x8, x11, x15, x20 |
| [[upgrade_efficiency]] | 4 | -15% FE per action, never below 40% |
| [[upgrade_range]] | 4 | bigger area, depends on the machine |
| [[upgrade_fortune]] | 3 | Fortune I-III (Looting in the Replicator) |
| [[upgrade_silk]] | 1 | blocks drop themselves; not with Fortune |
| [[upgrade_growth]] | 4 | +50% crop and sapling growth |
| [[upgrade_void]] | 1 | deletes junk or outputs that don't fit |
| [[upgrade_height]] | 6 | Architect Table: buildings one block taller |
| [[upgrade_carry]] | 1 | Storage Terminal keeps its items when picked up |

> Every speed card costs more FE per action than the one before: 1 card +30%, 8 cards x6.2. The quarries pay a steeper price: 8 cards cost 21x the FE per block in the [[excavator_mk4]].
