## Placing it

- The table becomes the middle floor block of the centre plot. Place it on flat ground at the floor height you want.
- The grid is 5x5 plots, 41x41 blocks, centred on the table. Neighbouring plots share a wall, so every room is 7x7 inside.
- Put a chest against the table first. Cleared and demolished items go into it, else they pile up on top of the table. A container touching the table is never cleared.

## Building

- With Clear terrain off, blocks in the way stay and the hall is built around them.
- Clear terrain only runs on a plot's first build. Later passes leave what you put inside alone.
- Clearing is slow. Flatten big hills and cut trees yourself first.
- It only builds while the area is loaded: stay close.
- Spawn protection and claims you can't build in are skipped.
- Your scoreboard team can use your table.

{{image shots/gui_architect_demolish.jpg|The Architect Table screen with Demolish}}

## Moving it

Breaking the table keeps its matter, energy and plan. Put back on the same block, it carries on. Placed somewhere else, it forgets what it built (the blocks stay) and builds the queued plots at the new spot.

## Pocket Architect

- It eats junk from anywhere in your inventory, off hand included.
- The junk list starts with cobblestone, cobbled deepslate, dirt, gravel, netherrack, tuff, diorite, andesite and granite.
- With a block picked, right-click in the air does nothing. Sneak-right-click to open it.
