Fit modules at a [[tinkers_bench]].

- FE gear takes no enchantments: modules replace them. The Age 0 tools (Tinker's Hammer, Felling Axe, Gearblade) still take enchantments.
- One of each kind per item. Level II and III are crafted from the level below and need a higher Age or Mk.
- Overclock, Fortune and Silk Touch only work while the tool has FE.
