Cards upgrade machines. Modules upgrade what you carry: power tools, FE weapons and Exo armor. FE gear takes no enchantments; the Age 0 tools (Tinker's Hammer, Felling Axe, Gearblade) still do.

{{items tinkers_bench overclock_module fortune_module sharpened_edge_module power_regulator_module}}

## Installing

1. Put the tool, weapon or Exo piece in a [[tinkers_bench]].
2. Drop modules in the open slots. Take one out to get it back.
3. G switches the modules of the tool or weapon in your hand, J those of your worn armor (J also installs).

- One of each kind per item. On Exo armor a kind works once per suit ([[capacitor_plating_module]] once per piece).
- Level II and III are crafted from the level below and need a higher Age or Mk.
- Every module starts switched on.

| Item | Slots |
|---|---|
| Power tools, FE weapons | Age 1 / 2 / 3 / 4: 2 / 3 / 4 / 5 |
| Exo pieces | Mk1 / 2 / 3 / 4: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 |

## Tool modules

| Module | Fits | Min. Age | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[overclock_module]] I-III | drills, Chainsaw | 1 / 2 / 3 | +50 / 100 / 200% mining speed, +20 / 40 / 60% FE |
| [[fortune_module]] I-III | drills | 1 / 2 / 3 | Fortune I-III on drops |
| [[silk_touch_module]] | drills | 1 | blocks drop themselves |
| [[auto_pickup_module]] | drills, Chainsaw | 1 | drops go into your inventory |
| [[void_filter_module]] | drills, Chainsaw | 1 | deletes junk drops |
| [[lamp_placer_module]] | drills | 1 | Spark Lamps where you mine in the dark, 10 FE each |

> B cycles Fortune, Silk Touch and off. Overclock, Fortune and Silk Touch only work while the tool has FE.

## Weapon modules

| Module | Fits | Min. Age | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[sharpened_edge_module]] I-III | FE weapons | 1 / 2 / 3 | +15 / 30 / 45% damage |
| [[looting_module]] I-III | FE weapons | 1 / 2 / 3 | Looting I-III on kills while the weapon has FE |
| [[thermal_edge_module]] | FE weapons | 1 | sets targets on fire |
| [[armor_pierce_module]] I-III | FE weapons | 1 / 2 / 3 | ignores 20 / 35 / 50% of armor |
| [[chain_lightning_module]] I-III | Arc Blade | 3 | more arcs, longer jumps |
| [[ricochet_module]] I-II | Rivet Gun | 2 | rivets bounce to 1 / 2 more monsters |
| [[lifesteal_module]] | FE weapons | 4 | heals 10% of damage dealt, from a 3-health pool |

## Everything

[[power_regulator_module]] I-III (Age or Mk 2 / 3 / 4) fits tools, weapons and Exo pieces: 15 / 25 / 35% less FE per block, per hit or for every module of the suit.
