title: Mob Farms

summary: Iron Golem and Wither farms: a 5x5x5 chamber that builds the mob from Essence Vials and strikes it down with Impact Rams.
---
1. Fill the vials: three Villager and one zombie for the [[iron_golem_farmer]], three Wither Skeleton for the [[wither_farmer]]. Sampling a villager does not hurt it.
2. Build the 5x5x5: the controller in a side wall, three [[impact_ram]]s in the middle of three side walls, the farm's matrix in the middle of the roof, at least one [[farm_port]], the chamber empty.
3. Put the vials in the controller and feed FE into a Farm Port. Pull the loot out of the same port.

- The vials are never used up.
- The Wither Farmer gives one [[minecraft:nether_star]] per Wither; its recipes take five.
- A [[plasma_actuator]] in the boost slot speeds it up for extra FE, as on the [[replicator_controller]].
