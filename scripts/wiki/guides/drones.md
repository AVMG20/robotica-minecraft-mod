## Power

- Put a cell or wound Mainspring in a drone's battery slot, or right-click the drone with one. The Hauler has no slot: right-click it.
- A [[wireless_charger]] charges every drone in range.
- Your scoreboard team can use your drones.

## Buddy Bot tunnels

- The tunnel runs level from your feet, in the compass direction you face. Looking up or down changes nothing, so stand at the height you want to mine.
- It flies over up to 24 blocks of open air before it starts digging.
- Both keys reach your bots up to 48 blocks away. Press the Buddy key again to stop a tunnel.
- Lava and water end the tunnel: it plugs them and comes back. With **Fill gaps** on and enough blocks, it bridges them instead.
- It digs around machines and chests, and stops at unloaded chunks.
- **Void junk** keeps a stack of junk for floors and plugs.
- Spark Lamps cost it FE only: no lamps needed in the cargo.

## Buddy Bot in a fight

- In every stance it leaves players, tamed pets and villagers alone.
- Its kills count as yours: XP and player-only drops.

## Courier routes

- The face you sneak-click counts. Click the side a machine gives items out of for the source, and the side it takes them in for the target. See [Item Pipes and Machine Sides](#/guide/logistics).
- Link routes with the drone item before you deploy it: they stay in the item.
- The remote works from up to 128 blocks away while the drone is loaded.
- It only flies when the target has room, and takes turns between its routes.
- A route with an end in an unloaded chunk waits.

## Hauler Drone

{{image shots/hauler_drone.jpg|Hauler Drones carrying a cow and a villager}}

- It lifts unridden mobs that are not bosses or other players' pets. Get a mob out of a boat or minecart first.
- Fly it over the spot and right-click: the mob goes down on the highest safe ground below.
