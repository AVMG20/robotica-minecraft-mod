## Tips

- Thorium is densest around its best Y and thins out towards both ends.
- Pyrolite also spawns in basalt and blackstone; basalt deltas get extra veins.
- Resonite needs the outer End islands: beat the dragon first.
- No time to dig? A [[survey_rig]] turns FE into ores.
