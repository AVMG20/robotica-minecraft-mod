## Tips

- Thorium is densest around its best Y and thins out towards both ends.
- Pyrolite also spawns in basalt and blackstone; basalt deltas get extra veins.
- Resonite needs the outer End islands: beat the dragon first.
- The [[grinder_mk1]] doubles every ore. A [[survey_rig]] turns FE into random ores without digging.

## Rusted Foundry

A ruined hall with a [[colossus_altar]] and a chest with a [[signal_flare]]. Light the flare at the altar to fight the Scrap Colossus for its [[servo_core]].
