Day one to the End

Hover an item. Every tier upgrades the last one.

Day one · copper only

Meet Stumpy

Wind up a spring, drop a robot near your trees and go explore.

  • Stumpy chops whole trees and replants
  • Sprout harvests and replants crops
  • Both fill your chests, or your Refined Storage
Wireless power

No cables. Just coils.

Put a Tesla Coil on a battery or generator, grab the Linker and click your machines. Chain coils to reach further.

  • 4 to 32 links per coil, up to 1M FE/t
  • Click a face to pick the side power goes in
  • Quiet when you're done, glowing when you hold the Linker
Power tools

1×1. 3×3. 12×12×12.

Press V to switch modes. Sneak for one block. A battery in your bag keeps the drill going.

Drones

Your own little crew

  • Mining Drone digs a 3×3 tunnel, lights it, and flies back if it smells lava
  • Sentry Drone guards your base or follows you into a fight
  • Courier Drone carries items from chest to chest
Base builder

Click a grid. Get a base.

Feed the Architect Table cobble and wood. Pick plots. It builds clean 9×9 halls, joins them and adds the doors.

Four styles, from clean stone to tech.

Storage Terminal

A chest that grew up

81 slots, search, sorting and a crafting grid. Add expansions for up to 648 slots. Hoppers and drones can use it too.

Warp

Portals, but cooler

One projector block throws a swirling portal into the air. Link two and walk through, even across dimensions.

Warp Pads and a Recall Remote get you home.

First boss

The Scrap Colossus

Find a Rusted Foundry, light a Signal Flare at the altar and dodge the slam. When it overheats, hit the glowing core.

  • Drops the Servo Core you need for Age 2
  • Summon it again whenever you want
  • Build your own altar for a home arena
Exo-Frame

Suit up

Powered armor with modules: speed, night vision, water breathing, double jump, a magnet, and real flight.

Mob Replicator

Bottle a monster

Sample a mob with a vial, put it in the replicator and get its loot. No mob farm, no lag.

No wiki needed

The Codex shows the way

Every new player gets a Codex. It tells you your next step, and every item in it shows its recipe.

Play it

Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge. Works next to Thermal, Mekanism, Refined Storage and AE2, shows up in JEI and Jade, and runs on servers.

1Install NeoForge 21.1 for Minecraft 1.21.1 from neoforged.net.
2Drop robotica-0.1.0.jar in your mods folder.
3Start the NeoForge profile, open your Codex and craft a Copper Gear.
