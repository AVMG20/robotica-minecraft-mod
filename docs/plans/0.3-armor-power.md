# 0.3 plan: Exo armor rework, industry and big power

Goal: a grind loop. Better armor and tools need more FE and machine-made parts; machine-made parts need power;
more power needs new ores, machines and multiblocks; those need the parts again. Every step is a resource sink.

**Modpack balance (binding).** Robotica is mostly played in packs next to Mekanism, Thermal, Immersive Engineering,
Create, Refined Storage, AE2. Those already do ore doubling, generic power and bulk processing. So:
- Do not compete on generic ore processing: the Crusher handles Robotica's own materials; vanilla ore doubling is
  absent or a server config that is off by default. Never beat other mods' doubling rates.
- Use `c:` tags for every input and output (ingots, dusts, plates, gems, ores, storage blocks) so other mods' items and
  machines interoperate both ways, and other mods' ores/dusts work in our recipes where sensible.
- FE numbers in line with those mods: early generators tens to low hundreds of FE/t, mid reactors thousands to tens of
  thousands, endgame hundreds of thousands. Our own consumers (armor, tools, machines) must stay usable with foreign
  power, so no Robotica-only energy.
- Robotica's unique pull is its own progression items (armor marks, modules, cores, alloys only our Alloy Smelter
  makes, parts only our Assembler makes). Make those the sink, not the generic stuff.
- Everything tunable in the server config.

Three workstreams run in parallel in separate worktrees (A, B, C). After they are merged, an integration pass wires
the cross-links (marked **[integration]** below) and an audit runs. Each stream reads `docs/AGENT_BRIEF.md` and
`docs/DESIGN.md` first; the brief's rules apply, with the shared-file exceptions listed per stream.

Shared-file rules for this round (all streams):
- `Robotica.java` / `RoboticaClient.java`: a new module may add its one `init` line. Nothing else.
- `docs/DESIGN.md`: write or rewrite only your own module's section (A: a new "Exo-Frame (module `exo`)" section; B: a
  new "Industry (module `industry`)" section; C: a new "Big energy (module `energy`)" section), placed after
  "Machines and power". Do not touch other sections.
- `scripts/data/codex_guide.py`: you may add guide steps and recipe unlocks for your own items, and move your own
  module's existing unlocks. Re-run the script. Keep the ladder sensible (an item unlocks at the step where its parts
  are available).
- `src/main/resources/assets/robotica/codex/chapters.json`: add or edit only your module's chapter text.
- No item of yours may be missing a recipe or obtain path; `everyitemhasarecipe` must pass.
- Tests: `scripts/docker-build.sh runGameTestServer` (Docker; there is no local JDK). All required tests must pass.
- Commit on your worktree branch with a plain imperative subject, no `Co-Authored-By`, no tool advertising. Do not push.

Ages for reference: 0 Clockwork (copper), 1 Wired (iron, redstone, gold), 2 Servo (diamond, obsidian, quartz,
Servo Core), 3 Deep (netherite, blaze, prismarine, Magma Core), 4 Antigrav (ender, nether star, shulker, Antigrav Core).

## A. Exo-Frame rework (module `exo`)

Today: Mk1 (iron-like, 1 module slot per piece) and Mk2 (diamond-like, 2 slots), 13 crafted module items installed in
the armor's own GUI (J, or sneak-use a piece), mostly on/off, a pooled battery when the full set is worn.

1. **Marks.** Add Mk3 (Age 3) and Mk4 (Age 4) by smithing, keeping components (energy, modules, toggles):
   Mk3 = Blazing Casing template + Mk2 piece + Plasma Actuator; Mk4 = Null Casing template + Mk3 piece + Null Circuit.
   Protection: Mk3 netherite-like (3/6/8/3, toughness 3, knockback resistance 0.1); Mk4 4/7/9/4, toughness 4, KB 0.2,
   fire-resistant item. Batteries x4 per mark. Module slots per piece: Mk1 1, Mk2 2, Mk3 3, Mk4 4.
   **[integration]** Mk3/Mk4 recipes will later also take industry alloys (pyrosteel / resonant alloy plates).
2. **Mark gating.** Each module kind gets a minimum mark (Flight Mk3 chest, Kinetic Shield Mk3, Hazard Seal Mk3, ...).
   The GUI refuses and explains.
3. **Module levels.** Modules that scale get levels I-III as separate items like Servo Stride today (upgrade recipe =
   previous level + next age's parts): Night Vision (II adds thermal sight: hostile mobs within 24 blocks glow for the
   wearer), Magnet (radius 6/10/16), Kinetic Shield (absorb 75/85/95%, cheaper FE per point), Spring Heels (jump boost
   I/II/III), Jet Assist, Fall Dampener (FE per block down), Med Injector. Same kind cannot be installed twice in the
   suit (fixes Stride I + II in one leggings).
4. **Step Assist on the boots**, Age 1, cheap: walk up full blocks (step height 1.0). The user loves it; keep it early.
5. **New modules** (pick slots sensibly, numbers in the server config):
   - Auto-Feeder (helmet, A1): eats food from your inventory when hunger drops.
   - Kinetic Generator (legs, A1): walking and sprinting charge the suit (small, a few FE per block).
   - Solar Weave (helmet, A2): recharges the suit in sunlight.
   - Sonar Pulse (helmet, A2, key): ping that outlines ores (`c:ores`) and mobs around you for a few seconds, radius by
     level; client-side highlight, server-side cost.
   - Med Injector (chest, A2, I-III): heals when health drops low, FE cost and cooldown.
   - Hydro Fins (boots, A2): swim speed and underwater mining speed.
   - Hazard Seal (chest, A3): clears poison, wither, hunger, nausea, blindness for FE.
   - Dash Thrusters (legs or boots, A3, key): short horizontal dash, cooldown.
   - Capacitor Plating (any piece, I-III): +50/100/200% battery for that piece.
   - Power Regulator (any piece, I-III): -10/20/30% FE for every module in the suit.
6. **Core socket.** Chestplates Mk2+ get one core slot (core not consumed, removable). Bonus needs all four pieces at
   Mk2+ worn: Servo Core = Overclock (key: haste II + speed for 10 s, 60 s cooldown, FE cost); Magma Core = fire
   immunity, burning melee hits, lava does not slow you; Antigrav Core = no fall damage, Flight costs half, Dash
   cooldown halved. This gives boss cores a lasting use.
7. **UI.** Rebuild `ExoScreen`: opened with J it shows all four worn pieces (rows or a paper-doll layout), up to 4
   module slots per piece, level pips, per-module toggle, tooltip with FE cost and requirement, pooled energy bar plus
   per-piece bars, the core socket. Sneak-use on a held piece still opens that piece alone. Lift the hard-coded 2-slot
   limits (`ExoMenu.readSections`, button ids, `ExoScreen` layout, `ExoItems.piece`).
   HUD: compact suit energy bar, icons of active modules, cooldown rings (Overclock, Dash, Med Injector), sonar
   results. Client config to move or hide it.
8. **Juice.** Jet and flight particles and a loop sound, shield hit sparks and sound, dash whoosh, sonar ping sound,
   injector hiss. Mk3 and Mk4 armor textures and new module icons via `scripts/textures/exo.py`.
9. Keys: keep J (GUI) and K (flight). New default keys must not clash with V, B, G (gear), J, K or vanilla defaults.
10. Tests: marks/slots/gating, levels and no-duplicate rule, core socket bonus, Capacitor Plating capacity, Step Assist.

## B. Industry: ores, materials, processing machines (module `industry`)

New module for the resource and machine layer. Power is the currency: every machine eats FE and takes speed /
efficiency cards (speed costs FE steeply, as `Upgrades.energyMultiplier`).

1. **Ores and worldgen** (biome modifiers + configured/placed features, data generated by a script):
   - Thorium (Overworld, stone and deepslate variants, around Y -48..32, common enough to mine by hand): raw thorium,
     raw block, ingot, block, dust, plate (`c:` tags: `ores/thorium`, `raw_materials/thorium`, `ingots/thorium`,
     `dusts/thorium`, `plates/thorium`, `storage_blocks/...`).
   - Pyrolite (Nether, netherrack, richer in basalt deltas): pyrolite shard (gem), dust.
   - Resonite (End, end stone on the outer islands, rare): resonite crystal, dust.
   Survey Rig and Excavator pick them up through `c:ores` automatically.
2. **Machines** (block entities, `MachineMenu`/`MachineScreen`, battery slot, upgrade slots, item capability on all
   sides with sensible input/output faces, progress bar, FE/t readout):
   - Crusher (A1): Robotica ores and raw materials to dust (x2), alloys back to dust, coal to graphite dust. Vanilla
     ore doubling only behind a server config, off by default (packs have their own).
   - Alloy Smelter (A1): two inputs to one alloy. Ferrothorium (iron + thorium, A2), Pyrosteel (ferrothorium +
     pyrolite + blaze powder, A3), Resonant Alloy (pyrosteel + resonite + ender pearl, A4); ingots, and plates through
     the Metal Press / hammer (`c:plates/...`).
   - Centrifuge (A2): depleted fuel pellet -> thorium dust (about half back) + chance of Radiant Isotope; also other
     separations you find fun (e.g. magma cream -> slime + blaze powder).
   - Assembler (A2): machine-only crafting of advanced parts from plates/circuits for less raw material than the
     crafting table, plus parts that exist only here (e.g. Thermocouple, Superconductor Coil A3, Resonant Lattice A4).
   Recipes are data-driven (your own recipe types + JEI categories in `compat` are welcome; if you add JEI support,
   keep it in your module and note it).
3. **RTG** (Radioisotope Generator, A2, single block): holds thorium fuel pellets, quiet passive FE (about 150 FE/t per
   pellet for a long time), the first step past the Combustion Generator. Stackable by building more.
4. **Fuel items** for the reactor in stream C: thorium fuel pellet (A2), enriched fuel pellet (A3: pellet + pyrolite +
   magma cream), depleted fuel pellet (waste), fusion fuel pellet (A4: resonite + radiant isotope).
   Write their stats into the data maps defined by stream C (see contract below):
   `src/main/resources/data/robotica/data_maps/item/reactor_fuel.json` and `.../fusion_fuel.json`.
5. **Generator cards.** The Combustion Generator and Solar Panels accept speed and efficiency cards: speed raises FE/t
   and fuel burn, efficiency raises FE per fuel item. You may edit those power-module classes for this (only this).
6. Tests: crusher doubling, alloy recipe, RTG output and pellet use, generator cards, worldgen features registered.

## C. Big energy: multiblocks, reactor, capacitor bank (module `energy`)

1. **Multiblock framework** in `core/multiblock` (you may create this core package; nothing else in core): a
   variable-size cuboid structure detected from a controller (frame, walls with allowed blocks, interior predicate,
   min/max size from config), cheap re-validation when member blocks change (members remember the controller, or a
   throttled re-scan), and a precise error for the GUI (first wrong block position and why). Reusable by later
   multiblocks.
2. **Fission Reactor** (A2-A3): cuboid 3x3x3 up to 7x7x7 exterior (config). Reactor Casing frame, Reactor Glass in
   walls, exactly one Reactor Controller in a wall, Power Ports (FE out, Tesla coils can sit on them), Access Ports
   (items in/out, automation friendly). Interior: Fuel Rod blocks in full vertical columns, the rest air or coolant
   blocks. Coolant values come from the block data map `robotica:reactor_coolant` (water 1, ice 1.5, packed ice 2,
   blue ice 3, plus your own Cryo Coolant block). Heat from burning fuel becomes FE; coolant next to rods raises
   efficiency; too little cooling raises temperature, throttles output and finally SCRAMs (shuts down, no explosion,
   no block damage). Control-rod insertion 0-100% from the GUI to throttle. Each pellet burned yields its waste item to
   the access port. GUI: temperature gauge, fuel and waste bars, FE/t, rod slider, structure status. Target scale: a
   small reactor around 1,000 FE/t, a 7x7x7 with good coolant and enriched fuel in the tens of thousands.
3. **Capacitor Bank** (energy storage): cuboid 3x3x3 up to 9x9x9 (config). Bank Casing frame, Bank Glass, Bank
   Controller (GUI), Bank Ports (each set to input or output). Interior: any mix of Capacitor blocks (storage) and
   Transfer Coils (I/O rate), rest air. Capacitors: Copper (from copper cells, ~8M), Redstone (~64M), Ender (~512M).
   Transfer coils: Basic 64k, Advanced 512k, Elite 4M FE/t. Energy stored as a long in the controller; ports clamp to
   int. GUI: big gauge, stored / capacity, average in / out FE/t, a 60 s history sparkline, structure status.
   **[integration]** a Resonant Capacitor (resonite) tier later.
4. **Fusion Reactor** (A4, stretch goal after 1-3): a large fixed-shape structure (e.g. a ring of Fusion Coils with
   casing, glass, controller and ports). Ignition needs a big FE charge (around 20M FE, a deliberate sink), then it
   burns fusion fuel for around 100k-250k FE/t. Uses the `robotica:fusion_fuel` item data map.
5. Recipes use existing ladder parts for now. **[integration]** later switch casings to industry plates.
6. Tests: structure detection (valid, too small, wrong block, missing controller), reactor heat/FE/scram with a test
   fuel, bank capacity and I/O clamp, long storage beyond int.

### Data map contract (C defines, B fills)
- `robotica:reactor_fuel` (items): `{"heat": int, "ticks": int, "waste": "<item id>" (optional)}`. `heat` is reactor
  heat per tick while one unit burns, roughly FE/t at efficiency 1.0. Intended values: thorium pellet heat 400 /
  12,000 ticks; enriched pellet heat 1,000 / 12,000 ticks; both waste `robotica:depleted_fuel_pellet`.
- `robotica:fusion_fuel` (items): `{"power": int, "ticks": int}` (FE/t while burning). Fusion pellet ~200,000 / 2,400.
- `robotica:reactor_coolant` (blocks): `{"cooling": float}`.
C ships only the coolant JSON; B ships the two fuel JSONs. C's tests must not depend on B's items.
