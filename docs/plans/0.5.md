# 0.5 plan: Nether boss, solar tiers, balance pass, screenshots

Three streams in worktrees, then merge, audit, fixes. Read CLAUDE.md. Work alone (no subagents), short tool calls.
Update the Codex, guide unlocks, wiki sources (rebuild the wiki) and DESIGN.md for everything you change.

## A. Nether boss (module `boss`)
Replace the temporary Magma Core recipe with a Nether boss, like the Scrap Colossus does for the Servo Core
(read the `boss` module: Colossus, altar, Signal Flare, Rusted Foundry, loot lock, fairness rules).
- An Age 3 boss in the Nether (e.g. a molten forge construct) with its own summon (altar or item), a structure where
  players find it or its summon, two or three readable attack patterns, a weak window, and fairness rules (only living
  attackers, no cheese, loot locked to the killer for a while).
- Drops the Magma Core (plus fitting extras). Keep `temp_magma_core` as a pricier fallback recipe for servers that
  turn the structure off or play Peaceful, like `temp_servo_core`.
- Model, textures, sounds (vanilla events), boss bar, guide step, Codex, wiki, tests. The End boss comes later.

## B. Solar tiers, balance pass, config-true tooltips (modules `power`, `core`, others for balance)
- Solar Panel Mk3 and Mk4 (Age 3 and 4). Easy passive power with no input: never close to reactors. Mk1 20 and Mk2 80
  FE/t stay; pick Mk3/Mk4 so a Mk4 panel is useful but a small fraction of a 5x5x5 fission reactor. No cards.
- Balance pass: run `python3 scripts/cost_report.py`, review `docs/COSTS.md` and the configs for 0.3/0.4 content
  (industry, energy, processing, modules, machines). Fix outliers against the component ladder and against
  Mekanism/Thermal norms. Specifically decide the Combustion Generator efficiency gain (4 cards = 2.5x FE per fuel)
  and make Fortune / Silk Touch / Looting modules need a charged tool or weapon like Overclock does.
- Every tooltip, Codex or wiki line that shows a configurable number reads the config value (e.g. the upgrade-cards
  caps tooltip), not hard-coded defaults.
